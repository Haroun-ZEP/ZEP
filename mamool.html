01 . 20 . 2005

Chapter 1 — The Sardi

Mamool is a Sardi sheep, a famous Moroccan sheep breed. His appearance is unmistakable: predominantly white wool, black markings around the eyes and face, a black nose, black hooves, and enormous curved horns.

His horns curl around like demon horns, forming an imposing near-circle before continuing into long, powerful horns.

Mamool was not an ordinary Sardi. He was considered the strongest Sardi in the Moroccan Kingdom.

Mamool was raised in Saudi Arabia but had Moroccan Sardi DNA. His family history stretched back generations.

His grandfather was said to have been alive during the lifetime of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Chapter 2 — The Escape

Mamool originally lived in Morocco.

Eventually, he escaped Morocco.

His disappearance became significant because Mamool became the first Sardi documented outside Morocco.

Chapter 3 — The Fate

01 . 20 . 2005

Winter.

A strange night would become one of the earliest major events connected to Mamool.

This event became known as The Fate.

Mamool was the strongest Sardi in the Moroccan Kingdom, and because of this he was chosen to be the first sacrifice.

Chapter 4 — Abuld

Abuld was an old Z.E.P detective. He was essentially the 2005 equivalent of what Raul would later become.

During a cold winter night, Abuld was called into a snowy forest.

Wearing his jacket, he walked through the forest alone.

Then he saw something.

A sheep stood in the snow.

Its face was white, with dark markings surrounding its eyes. Its nose and mouth were black, and its thick winter wool made it appear almost like a guardian of the forest.

Abuld did not know what he had discovered.

He did not know that the animal was a Sardi. He did not know that it was the first Sardi documented outside Morocco.

Chapter 5 — The Photograph

Abuld managed to take a photograph of the mysterious sheep.

He kept the photograph.

That photograph would eventually become an important piece of evidence connected to the mystery surrounding Mamool.

Chapter 6 — Abuld's Fate

Abuld did not simply walk away from the encounter.

He was eventually found dead.

Part of his body was missing, and the truth was eventually connected to Mamool.

Mamool had eaten Abuld.

Chapter 7 — The Investigation

Mamool was eventually taken by force to Haroun so that Z.E.P could examine him.

At this point, Mamool was extremely dangerous and violent.

Z.E.P had encountered something that was not behaving like an ordinary sheep.

Chapter 8 — Mamool Joins Z.E.P

Despite Mamool's dangerous nature, Z.E.P eventually decided not to abandon him.

Instead, they made him one of their own.

Mamool became a Z.E.P pet.

The terrifying sheep from the forest had somehow become part of the organization.

Chapter 9 — The Police Sheep

Mamool eventually became the official police sheep of Z.E.P.

He was paired with Pepsi Man.

The two became one of the strangest partnerships in Z.E.P.

Chapter 10 — The Mystery Continues

The story of Mamool does not end with his recruitment.

The snowy forest, Abuld's photograph, The Fate, Mamool's origins, and the November Polar Bear remain connected to the larger mystery.
