🎭 The Raul Arc

"The Fool"

The story of Raul and the events that shaped his beginning with Z.E.P.

Chapter 1 — The Beginning

September 26, 2001

Raul was only 2 years old.

On September 26, 2001, a strange black fog appeared over Damyo Town. It rose like a hurricane, moved with the power of a tornado, and traveled across the town like a sandstorm.

Chaos spread through Damyo Town.

Raul somehow survived.

He had survived by only 1%.

Raul lost both of his parents and was left alone.

Despite being only two years old, Raul began walking.

He walked for nearly 20 miles until he reached Z.E.P York.

But Z.E.P York was not safe.

A war was happening there.

Among the chaos, Raul saw someone lying on the ground.

It was Haroun.

Haroun had lost his legs and was struggling to survive.

Raul had almost nothing left.

But he still gave Haroun his last piece of food.

Raul then continued on.

Eventually, Saci found him.

Saci took Raul to the Z.E.P headquarters.

At that time, the headquarters was only 10 feet tall.

Saci gave Raul food and brought him inside.

Chapter 2 — The Badge

Raul eventually woke up inside the 10-foot Z.E.P headquarters.

Around him were detectives doing their usual work.

Raul watched them carefully.

Then he noticed something.

A Z.E.P detective badge.

Raul picked it up.

Saci noticed.

Saci expected Raul to give it back.

Raul refused.

Instead, he pointed at the badge.

Saci slowly realized what Raul wanted.

Raul wanted to become a detective.

Chapter 3 — The New Headquarters

October

It was a normal October day.

Haroun was still recovering while everyone else was doing their usual stupid things around the headquarters.

Suddenly, one of the supposed "detectives" ripped open his shirt.

He wasn't a detective.

He was a terrorist.

"EVERYBODY MOVE!"

He revealed a bomb and threw it to the ground.

The explosion destroyed the entire 10-foot headquarters.

Everyone managed to escape.

Z.E.P contacted the Police Department.

SWAT arrived and stopped the terrorist.

After the incident, the Police Department offered to help Z.E.P build a new headquarters.

And this time...

It would be enormous.

The new Z.E.P headquarters would stand 80 feet tall.

Chapter 4 — The COPE Broadcast

One day, Raul suddenly found himself being chased by a demon.

Or at least, that's what he thought.

The "demon" was actually Saci wearing a cardboard demon costume.

Raul eventually escaped and noticed something strange on a television station.

A strange message appeared.

"QUOTA ERROR"

Raul immediately ran to Den and showed her.

But when they checked the television...

It was just SpongeBob.

Then the broadcast began glitching.

Strange images appeared across the screen.

The reason was eventually discovered.

The terrorist organization known as The COPE had hijacked the broadcast and exported SpongeBob into the signal.

Raul continued screaming.

Chapter 5 — The Fool

December

It was a normal December evening.

Raul was suddenly chased by two people who looked like clowns.

They were actually two of the most-wanted serial killers.

They intended to capture Raul and sell his body parts on the black market.

Raul ran.

Then something impossible happened.

Pure white lightning suddenly rushed toward him.

Raul blacked out.

At the same moment, Z.E.P detectives arrived and moved toward the attackers.

In the story's canon, God sent an angel to protect Raul and give the detectives the opportunity to rescue him.

Raul later experienced a strange dream.

In the dream, an angel spoke to him.

"You got fooled, Almost fooled, its your turn to fool them back."

Raul woke up.

But instead of simply escaping...

Raul decided to fool everyone.

He allowed the killers to believe they had captured him.

Then Raul used an opening to strike one of them and fatally defeat him.

The second killer ran away.

Raul had survived.

He had been fooled.

Almost fooled.

But now...

It was his turn to fool them back.
